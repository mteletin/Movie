# LONG DUSK (長い黄昏) — production bible & technical contract

An HTML "film", about 37 minutes long. It plays in a browser: a canvas for visuals, Web Audio for the score and SFX, and subtitles for dialogue.
Tone: old-school anime (Legend of the Galactic Heroes, Patlabor 2, Royal Space Force, Cowboy Bebop's quiet episodes).
Character first. Politics are the weather the characters live in. **Do not over-explain.** Nobody says what they feel outright. Exposition is never spoken for the audience's sake. Silences carry weight. Images hold for a long time. Subtext does the work.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
## 1. WORLD (never stated in full on screen)

- **Orrin**: a banded gas giant (amber and violet bands, thin pale rings).
- **Vesper**: a large rotating cylinder habitat in orbit around Orrin. It is the seat of the **Concordat**, the old inner government: navy, ivory and gold, formal, aristocratic, bureaucratic. Vesper has no sun of its own. A ring of enormous **mirror petals** catches and focuses light into the cylinder. Opening the petals makes "dawn" and closing them makes "night". Daylight is political: the Mirror Authority is military.
- **The Shelf**: the outer mining colonies on Orrin's ice moons and in the rings. They haul ice and volatiles to Vesper. Their colors are rust, ochre and ice blue. Hard, poor, communal people who sing work songs.
- **The Orrin Accord**: the ceasefire treaty signed 12 years ago after the **War of the Shelf**. Its worst battle was **Callisto Gap**. The Accord is now up for renewal. Hawks on BOTH sides want it to fail.
- **The Long Dusk**: after the Incident (scene 7), the mirrors lock closed in emergency mode and Vesper lives in violet twilight for days.

## 2. CHARACTERS (visual design + voice)

**ILSE VARGA** (44). The Concordat's chief negotiator. Widow.
Look: dark auburn hair cut at the jaw, grey eyes with tired lids, a charcoal high-collared Concordat coat, a small silver pin at the collar.
Voice: precise and dry. Short sentences. Deflects with irony. Never raises her voice. Smokes illegal cigarettes on her balcony; open flame is a crime on Vesper.
Wound: her husband Daniel died at Callisto Gap. She has not forgiven Kasimir for surviving, or for leaving.

**MIRA VARGA** (17). Ilse's daughter.
Look: the same auburn hair, long and messily tied, an oversized olive-green work jacket (it is Juno's), one small silver earring.
Voice: quick and sharp. She tests people. She believes words can change things and wants that to be true.
She spends her nights at the docks with Juno and Shelf kids.

**KASIMIR ODUYA** (46). The Shelf's lead delegate. He was once a Concordat officer, Daniel's best friend and wingman at Callisto Gap. He defected to the Shelf after the war.
Look: dark skin, close-cropped hair flecked with grey, a scar along the left jaw, a rust and ochre Shelf coat with a pale fur collar.
Voice: warm and slow. Humor arrives late. He says less than he knows. He loved Ilse. That was never resolved and is never named.

**ADMIRAL HALE SEVERIN** (68). Commander of the Mirror Authority and the Concordat fleet. The hawk.
Look: white hair swept back, a white mustache, a navy uniform with gold, reading glasses. Gardening gloves in the garden.
Voice: courteous, grandfatherly, patient, terrifying. He is not a cartoon villain. His only son, **Lt. Aurel Severin**, died at Callisto Gap in the same battle as Daniel. He grows tomatoes under grow-lamps. He believes the Shelf will rearm and that only strength keeps the peace.

**JUNO ACHTERBERG** (34). Captain of the small freighter *Kestrel Moth*. She smuggles between the two sides.
Look: blonde hair with shaved sides, freckles, a patched flight jacket. She chews licorice root.
Voice: blunt and funny. Her older brother died in the war; she never says which side he fought for. She is the closest thing Mira has to an older sister.

**TEO MARSH** (19). A Shelf boy who works the haulers and lives illegally on Vesper.
Look: thin, black curly hair, frostbite-scarred fingers, a grey thermal top.
Voice: shy, then fierce. Sings Shelf work songs. He is in love with Mira, a little, and she half-knows it.

**DANIEL VARGA**: dead. Seen only in photographs and in memories. Blond and smiling, in a Concordat pilot's uniform.

**ANSEL ROE**: Kasimir's deputy and a Shelf hardliner. Mentioned only. He has been in secret contact with Severin's staff.

**Minor voices** (subtitle `who` values): `CHAIR` (treaty chair), `AIDE`, `GUARD`, `NEWS`, `RADIO`, `OFFICER`, `PA` (public address), `CHILD` (a child's voice on the radio).

## 3. KEY OBJECTS / MOTIFS

- **The cigarette and lighter.** Ilse's lighter was Daniel's. In the last scene she gives Kasimir the lighter.
- **The tomato.** Severin gives Ilse a tomato in scene 3. In the dusk the plants wilt. In scene 16 he offers a tomato to the officers who come to arrest him.
- **Letters.** After Daniel died, Kasimir wrote Ilse a letter. She never answered. Callback lines, used VERBATIM:
  - s05, Kasimir: "I wrote to you once." / Ilse: "I know. I kept it."
  - s17, Mira: "Will you write?" / Ilse: "I'm not good at answering." / Mira: "Then practice on me."
  - s18, Ilse: "I read it every year. I never knew what to say." / Kasimir: "You didn't have to say anything. You just had to not burn it."
- **Light.** Dawn in s01, the long dusk in s07 through s17, dawn returning in s18.
- **The Shelf work song** "Cold Hands Haul". Teo sings fragments in s04 and s13. Juno hums it in s17. Lyric fragments: "Cold hands haul, cold hands bring / ice for the kings who never sing".
- **Severin's line about Callisto Gap**, in s12: "We buried the same war, Ilse. I just kept digging."

## 4. PLOT TRUTH (what really happened, revealed only in fragments)

Mira, Teo and Juno plan a harmless protest. During the treaty reception they will hijack the mirror array's alignment controls for 90 seconds and tilt the petals to "write" a line of light across Vesper's sky: the names of the Shelf dead. Words only.

Someone swaps the device's code package. Juno's dockmaster was paid to hand her the altered package. The swap was arranged by Severin's staff with Ansel Roe's knowledge: each side's hawks feed the other.
The altered code does not tilt the petals. It unlocks one petal's restraints at dawn. The petal tears loose, swings into the agricultural ring and the fuel lines of a docked ice hauler, and starts a fire. **31 dead.** The mirrors lock closed and the Long Dusk begins.

Teo is arrested. He is Shelf, so it looks like Shelf terrorism. Talks are suspended and the fleet mobilizes.

Ilse learns that Mira was involved. Kasimir gives Ilse proof of the hawks' contact: an intercepted transmission between Roe and Severin's office.
Severin offers Ilse a deal: he buries Mira's role if Ilse lets the talks collapse.

Mira wants to confess so that Teo doesn't hang. Ilse finds the third way. At the final session she publicly states that the protest device came from Concordat hands, from her own household. She takes the responsibility on herself ("I knew. I said nothing."), which is half true. She puts the intercepted transmission into the record without naming Severin. Then she resigns. Kasimir signs the renewal. Severin walks out.
Teo is released. Mira leaves for the Shelf with Juno. Severin is arrested quietly in his garden. The mirrors reopen. Dawn.

The audience should be able to assemble all of this, but no character ever explains it in one speech.

## 5. SCENE LIST (id, duration in seconds, beats)

ACT I — 朝の光 MORNING LIGHT
- **s01** 100s. Cold open. Orrin rises. The Vesper exterior, then the mirror petals open: dawn. Ilse on her balcony, lighting an illegal cigarette with Daniel's lighter. Mira comes in from the night, through the door (she was out). A cold, short exchange. Mira goes to her room. Ilse looks at Daniel's photo. TITLE CARD "LONG DUSK / 長い黄昏".
- **s02** 150s. Treaty Hall, opening session. Formal speech fragments from the CHAIR and from Ilse. The Shelf delegation enters, Kasimir last. Ilse and Kasimir see each other for the first time in 12 years. They hold the look. Severin watches from the gallery. Kasimir's speech is short and quietly devastating ("We have brought ice. We always bring ice."). Corridor after: Kasimir says "Ilse." She keeps walking: "Delegate Oduya."
- **s03** 120s. Severin's hydroponic garden. Ilse visits. He prunes tomatoes and gives her one. Talk of Shelf rearmament ("They're building hulls on Thale."). She declines to see his file. As she leaves: "Your daughter keeps interesting company, Ilse. The docks are cold at night." Hold on Ilse's face.
- **s04** 130s. *Kestrel Moth* hold, night. Juno, Mira and Teo around the device. Juno hands over the code package: "Dockmaster says it's clean." Their plan comes in fragments ("ninety seconds", "names, just names"). Teo sings two lines of "Cold Hands Haul". Juno and Mira alone in the cockpit: Juno's brother, mentioned sideways. Mira: "Does it change anything? Words?" Juno: "Not usually. Do it anyway."
- **s05** 140s. Reception terrace at night: lanterns, glass. Ilse and Kasimir alone at last. Daniel is circled around, never addressed head on. The verbatim letter lines. Kasimir: "There are people on my side who want this to fail. Not only on yours." He leaves. Ilse looks up at the dark mirrors.

ACT II — 鏡 THE MIRROR
- **s06** 120s. Maintenance spine of the mirror array at night. Mira and Teo crawl with flashlights and install the device. A RADIO voice (Juno) counts. The device LEDs show a code signature Mira doesn't recognize. She hesitates: "These aren't the numbers." Teo: "Juno checked." She closes the panel. A small hand on hers.
- **s07** 110s. THE INCIDENT, at dawn. The petals open. One petal swings wrong and tears loose. Light spears sideways. It hits the agricultural ring and a docked hauler: fire. Minimal subtitles: PA announcements and alarms. Then the mirrors slam closed. Violet darkness. Card: "THE LONG DUSK". Mira on the docks, watching the fire, not moving.
- **s08** 130s. Emergency council session, red light. Severin speaks, calm and reasonable: suspend the talks, arm the fleet. Ilse asks for 72 hours. Severin grants them with a smile ("Seventy-two hours. The dark will wait."). NEWS fragments: "31 confirmed", "Shelf national detained". Kasimir is placed in a detention suite.
- **s09** 120s. Ilse's apartment, emergency lighting. Mira comes home. Ilse already knows. A long silence, then tea. Mira: "It was supposed to be words. Only words." Ilse doesn't hold her, then does. Ilse, quietly: "Who gave you the codes?" Mira can't answer. Ilse's hand on the lighter.
- **s10** 130s. Juno in the *Kestrel Moth* cockpit, alone. She replays the code package and finds a Concordat security signature buried in it. She goes to the dockmaster's office. It is empty, he's gone. RADIO: Teo has been arrested. Juno hits the console once, then sits very still. She chews the licorice root until it breaks.

ACT III — 黄昏 DUSK
- **s11** 140s. Detention suite. Ilse visits Kasimir. She tells him about Mira without saying the words. He gives her a data chip: Roe's transmission. "Your admiral and my deputy have been writing to each other." Memory flash of Daniel, Kasimir and young Ilse laughing. Kasimir: "Daniel would have known what to do." Ilse: "Daniel would have done the wrong thing beautifully." Their hands, briefly.
- **s12** 120s. Severin's garden in the dark. The tomatoes are wilting. Ilse confronts him. He neither confirms nor denies ("I didn't kill those people. A girl with a soldering iron did."). The offer: Mira's name disappears if the talks die. He shows her his son's helmet. "We buried the same war, Ilse. I just kept digging." She leaves without answering.
- **s13** 100s. Interrogation room. Teo alone, bruised, singing "Cold Hands Haul" under his breath. A guard. Cut to the dock: Juno finds Mira. "They'll hang him for it, Mira. Shelf boy. It's a gift for them." Mira: "Then I'll tell them." Juno doesn't stop her.
- **s14** 110s. Ilse and Mira at the dark window at night. Mira says she will confess. The bind, never fully spelled out. Mira: "If I don't, he dies for me." Ilse: "If you do, they'll say the Shelf used you. There's no version where you're believed." A long silence. Ilse: "Let me carry it." Mira: "You can't carry everything." Ilse: "Watch me."

ACT IV — 長い黄昏 LONG DUSK
- **s15** 150s. The final session in the Treaty Hall under emergency light. Ilse at the podium. She speaks plainly: the device came from Concordat hands, from her own house. "I knew. I said nothing." The transmission goes into the record. Murmurs. She resigns. Kasimir signs. The CHAIR calls the vote. Severin rises from the gallery and walks out without a word. Ilse stands alone at the podium.
- **s16** 90s. Severin's garden. He sits among the wilted plants and holds his son's helmet. Boots arrive. OFFICER: "Admiral." He picks the one good tomato and offers it to them. "It's the last one. Somebody should eat it." Hold.
- **s17** 120s. The docks and *Kestrel Moth*. Teo is released and thin; Juno puts her jacket over him. Mira and Ilse say goodbye in very few words. The verbatim "write" lines. Mira boards. The ship undocks. Juno hums "Cold Hands Haul".
- **s18** 120s. Observation deck. The mirrors reopen and the first dawn arrives after the Long Dusk. Ilse and Kasimir. She lights the cigarette with Daniel's lighter, then gives him the lighter. The verbatim letter lines. Silence. The light grows. The last image is Orrin rising, as at the opening. Card: "終".
- **s19** 60s. End credits, played over stars and Orrin. The engine renders them. Writers do not write s19.

Total: 2260s, about 37.7 minutes.

## 6. TECHNICAL CONTRACT

All files are plain browser JS with no modules and no build step. They are loaded as classic `<script>`s in this order: `music.js`, `shots_env.js`, `shots_chars.js`, `script_a.js`, `script_b.js`, `engine.js`. Each file must be wrapped in an IIFE and touch only its own global namespace. No external network fetches of any kind (no image URLs, no audio files). Everything is procedural.

### 6.1 Script files (`script_a.js` = s01–s10, `script_b.js` = s11–s18)
```js
(function(){
  window.LD_SCRIPT = window.LD_SCRIPT || [];
  window.LD_SCRIPT.push({
    id: 's01', act: 1, actTitle: 'MORNING LIGHT', actTitleJp: '朝の光',
    title: 'Dawn',               // short scene name (chapter menu)
    duration: 100,               // seconds, MUST equal the bible
    shots: [ {t:0, shot:'orrin_rise', p:{pan:'up'}}, {t:14, shot:'vesper_exterior', p:{light:0.1}} ],
    music: [ {t:0, cue:'main_title', fade:4}, {t:60, cue:'silence', fade:6} ],
    sfx:   [ {t:31, sfx:'lighter'} ],
    lines: [ {t:40, d:3.2, who:'MIRA', text:'You\'re up.'} ],
    cards: [ {t:4, d:5, text:'VESPER HABITAT · ORRIN SYSTEM', style:'location'} ]
  });
})();
```
- `t` is seconds from the start of the scene. Arrays are sorted by `t`. The first shot is at t=0. A shot lasts until the next shot starts.
- **Shot pacing:** most shots are 4–14 s. Hold longer on faces in silence. Cut fast only in the incident.
- `p` is an optional parameter object. The params each shot understands are listed in 6.3. Shots ignore unknown params.
- `lines`: `d` is the display duration (about 2.5 s + 0.06 s per character). Lines must not overlap one another. `who` is a character key: `ILSE MIRA KASIMIR SEVERIN JUNO TEO DANIEL CHAIR AIDE GUARD NEWS RADIO OFFICER PA CHILD`. Use the `who` value `''` for unattributed sound captions, written like `text:'(distant alarm)'`; use these sparingly. Speech in italics (memories or voices over radio) gets `it:true`.
- `cards` styles: `location` (small lower-left caption), `title` (the big film title), `act` (an act card: text plus `jp` field), `chapter` (a centered small caption), `end` (終). Cards overlay the current shot.
- Dialogue density: roughly 35–55% of screen time has a subtitle. Leave real silences.
- Music: one cue at a time. A new cue crossfades over `fade` seconds. Use `silence` to fade out.

### 6.2 Music (`music.js`, global `window.LD_MUSIC`)
```js
LD_MUSIC.init(audioCtx, destinationNode)  // called once by engine after user gesture
LD_MUSIC.cue(name, fadeSeconds)          // crossfade to named cue; 'silence' fades out
LD_MUSIC.sfx(name)                        // one-shot sound effect
LD_MUSIC.update(sceneTimeSeconds)         // optional per-frame hook, may be no-op
LD_MUSIC.stopAll()                        // immediate stop (seeking)
```
Cue names: `main_title`, `ilse_theme`, `kasimir_theme`, `ilse_kasimir` (the two themes interwoven), `severin_theme`, `mira_theme`, `juno_theme`, `shelf_song` (the work song, hummed or synth-voiced), `hall_ceremony`, `reception_jazz`, `tension`, `incident`, `dusk_ambience`, `grief`, `resolve`, `finale`, `credits`, `silence`.
SFX names: `lighter`, `door`, `airlock`, `alarm`, `pa_chime`, `clink`, `footsteps`, `radio_static`, `hum_stop`, `impact`, `fire_roar`, `heartbeat`, `teacup`, `console_beep`, `metal_creak`, `crowd_murmur`, `gavel`, `wind_vent`, `mirror_motor`.

### 6.3 Shots (`shots_env.js` and `shots_chars.js`, global registry `window.LD_SHOTS`)
```js
window.LD_SHOTS = window.LD_SHOTS || {};
LD_SHOTS['orrin_rise'] = function(ctx, w, h, t, p, G){ ... };
```
- `ctx` is a 2D canvas context, already cleared. `w`×`h` is the drawable area, always 16:9 (letterboxing is done by the engine). `t` = seconds since this shot began. `p` = params (may be `{}`). `G` = `{sceneT, globalT, dt, seed}`.
- Draw the full frame every call. Must be deterministic for a given `(t, p)`: seed any randomness with a fixed seed, not `Math.random()` per frame, so that seeking looks identical. It must hold 60 fps: cache static layers in offscreen canvases keyed by `w,h` and params.
- Style: old-school 1980s/90s cel anime. Flat cel shading with one hard shadow tone. Painted gradient backgrounds. Limited animation: slow camera pans across still paintings, hair or smoke that moves a little, blinking eyes. Strong silhouettes and rim light. The engine adds film grain, vignette and letterbox, so don't add them yourself.
- Common param: `pan`: `'left'|'right'|'up'|'down'|'in'|'out'|'none'` (default: a gentle drift). `dark: true` means emergency-light or long-dusk palette (violet and teal, with amber emergency strips).

**shots_env.js — environments and space (no faces):**
`black`, `stars`, `orrin_rise` (gas giant rising over the frame, bands slowly drifting; `p.light` 0..1 tints warmer), `vesper_exterior` (the cylinder with its ring of mirror petals; `p.light` 0..1 sets how open and bright the petals are; `p.damaged` shows a missing petal and debris; `p.dark`), `mirror_petals_close` (huge reflective panels turning, catching light; `p.opening` animates the opening over the shot), `mirror_tear` (THE INCIDENT, animated over about 8 s: a petal shears, a spear of light sweeps, debris), `fire_agri_ring` (fire and smoke against the curved interior farmland seen across the cylinder), `shelf_haulers` (an ice hauler convoy against Orrin's rings), `kestrel_moth_exterior` (a small boxy worn freighter at dock; `p.empty`, `p.dark`), `council_chamber_emergency` (red-lit tiers, silhouettes), `severin_garden` (hydroponic rows of tomato vines under magenta-white grow lamps; `p.dark` for lamps on emergency power, `p.wilted`), `maintenance_spine` (a narrow ribbed tunnel, flashlight cones, `p.alarm` red strobe), `device_closeup` (a hand-built circuit box with blinking LEDs and a small screen of hex; `p.headline` string to render), `reception_terrace` (night terrace with lanterns, glass railing, the habitat's curve beyond, blurred crowd silhouettes), `corridor` (a long curved corridor with ribs of light; `p.dark`), `freighter_cockpit_empty` (cluttered cockpit with no people, consoles glowing, a licorice root on the dash), `interrogation_room` (bare table, a single lamp cone; `p.withTeo` draws Teo's silhouette hunched at the table), `callisto_gap_memory` (a desaturated war memory: fighter silhouettes, flashes, debris, sepia/teal, scratchy), `credits_bg` (slow stars plus a dim Orrin).

### 6.4 Engine (`engine.js`, `index.html`): written by the director
The engine handles the player UI, the timeline, seeking, chapters, subtitles, cards, grain, letterbox and credits.
